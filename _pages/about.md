Hello! I am a PhD candidate in Chemical Engineering at Yale University and a member of the [Computational Soft Matter Group](https://haji-akbari.yale.edu/).

My research uses molecular dynamics simulations, rare-event sampling, high-throughput analysis, and machine learning to study how ice forms and grows. I am especially interested in the molecular mechanisms that govern ice nucleation and in designing and characterizing antifreeze proteins and peptide mimics.

Research interests
======

- Ice nucleation and crystal growth
- Antifreeze proteins and biomolecular interfaces
- Molecular dynamics and enhanced sampling
- Statistical thermodynamics and soft matter
- Machine learning for molecular simulation
